## Purpose

This matrix declares the single owner of every business concept in V.I.S.I.O.N. No concept is orphaned. No concept has competing owners. No concept is co-owned.

**Violation of this matrix is a violation of the Constitution (Article II).**

## Ownership Rules

```
RULE 1: Every business concept has exactly ONE owner.
RULE 2: The owner is declared in this matrix.
RULE 3: Ownership never changes without architectural review.
RULE 4: No system may duplicate another system's ownership.
RULE 5: Consumers read from owners — they never become owners.
RULE 6: Owners may delegate reads but never delegate ownership.
RULE 7: New concepts must declare an owner before activation.
RULE 8: Orphaned concepts (no owner) are not allowed.
RULE 9: Mutation rights are explicit — only the owner may mutate.
RULE 10: Propagation rights are governed by CBSS.
RULE 11: Review rights are governed by the Constitution (Article V).
```
